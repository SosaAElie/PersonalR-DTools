BioTech R&D Tools

Follow the Steps Below

  1. Edit your 96 Well Template.
    1. Download the template here: 96 Well Template.
    2. Prefix unknowns & controls with "Sample" & standards with "Standard" followed by a hyphen i.e. Standard-2000ug/mL.
    3. Empty wells should be labeled as "None".
    4. Repeated names will be considered replicates & their values averaged.
    5. The unit of the standards should all be the same, i.e ug/mL, mg/mL, ug/uL, etc.
  2. Export the data from the SoftMaxPro software as shown below:
  3. Upload the raw exported data and edited template.
  4. Enter the dilution factor of your samples.
  5. Select the regression model that will best fit your standards.
    1. Red text indicates unknowns that fell outside the standard curve.
    2. For standards that differ by a large magnitude, a logarithmic scale x-axis will allow better visualization
  6. Interpolate data and download the produced excel file.

Regression Model

Linear Regression

Most accurate when the dependent variable changes at a constant rate alongside the independent variable.

Logarithmic Regression

Most accurate when the dependent variable changes at a slow rate relative to the independent variable.

4-Parameter Logistic Equation

Accurate when data presents upper and lower asymptotes in a sigmodial shape.

5-Parameter Logistic Equation

Accurate when data presents upper and lower asymptotes in a sigmodial shape. When g = 1, reverts to 4PL.

Chart Settings

Linear X-Scale

X-axis is set assuming the x-values are close together.

Logarithmic X-Scale

X-axis is set assuming the x-values are far apart.

Hide Extrapolated?

Removes the unknowns that are outside the standard curve.
